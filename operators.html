Market value for gyms.

ShakeUp Mini is strongest when sold as a recovery amenity plus a measurable ancillary revenue line, not a novelty vending machine.

New non-dues revenue

Captures the post-workout purchase moment inside the facility instead of sending members to a cafe or convenience store.

Higher perceived service

Gives smaller gyms a premium nutrition touchpoint without hiring bar staff or building a smoothie counter.

Manager-friendly reporting

Monthly sales, uptime, and restock data make the program easier to renew, move, or expand.

Investor-ready story

Lease and hybrid plans turn the machine network into recurring revenue potential instead of one-time equipment sales.

Operational maturity: answer the failure cases upfront.

This is the section managers need before saying yes. It shows that ShakeUp has thought beyond the happy path.

Payment fails

User can retry, choose another wallet, or cancel. No drink starts before payment confirmation.

Ingredient low

Unavailable flavors should be hidden before order selection and flagged in the operator dashboard.

Cup or dispense issue

The UI should pause, explain the next step, and notify staff if automatic recovery fails.

Service visit

Cleaning, restock, and maintenance reminders keep the location from becoming an unmanaged vending corner.
